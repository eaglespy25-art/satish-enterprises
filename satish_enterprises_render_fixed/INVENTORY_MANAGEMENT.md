# Inventory Management

Built:
- Product create/edit
- Product variants
- Colour + size fields
- Price management
- Opening stock
- Manual stock adjustments
- Stock movement ledger
- Low-stock endpoint
- Row locking during manual stock changes
- Audit logging
- Real-time inventory change events

Recommended petticoat variants:
- XL
- XXL
- Black
- Red
- Yellow
- Other high-selling colours can be added later.

The system prevents manual stock from becoming negative. Payment fulfilment also uses a database lock/check before stock deduction.
